How to play Teen Patti Pro

Three cards, one round, highest hand wins the pot. config tpp-1.0.0

The round

  1. You are dealt three cards, face down.
  2. You have 30 seconds to place your bet. Seats A, B and C are highlighted while it is your turn.
  3. Betting closes and the hands are revealed.
  4. The best hand takes the pot. Ties split it.

Betting

Chip denominations are 20, 100, 500 and 1000. Minimum bet 20, maximum 100,000, and you can place up to 50 bets per round — which is how you raise after seeing what the others did.

On a phone: tap a seat to select it, tap again to bet. The selected seat highlights and the status line confirms the amount.

Hand rankings

Strongest first. When two hands are the same type, the tiebreak is the highest card in the hand.

RankHandWhat it is
1TrailThree of a kind. Ace-high trail beats everything below.
2Pure sequenceThree consecutive cards of the same suit — A,2,3 through Q,K,A.
3SequenceThree consecutive cards, mixed suits.
4ColourThree of the same suit, not in sequence.
5PairTwo of a kind. The third card is the kicker.
6High cardNothing above. Ace is high.

Example: a pair of Kings beats a pair of Fives; a King-high colour beats a pair; and a King-high sequence beats the same sequence in mixed suits.

Your avatar

The picture and frame under your seat come from the DearLive admin panel and are tied to your player ID. If you have not been styled yet, or the lookup cannot reach the server, you get the default icon — the game never stops for it.

Play responsibly. This is a game of chance. Only play with money you can afford to lose, and set your own limits.
